Waypage privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Waypage is an offline reader. It has no server of its own, no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your library stays on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you turn on one of the features below, and each one sends only what it needs.

What stays on your device

The pages, files, highlights, tags, collections, reading places and reading stats you keep in Waypage are stored on your device. Waypage doesn't collect them and can't see them.

Saving a page

When you save a page, your device downloads it straight from the site it's on, as a browser would. That site sees the request, as it would if you opened the page. Wikipedia pages come through Wikipedia's own API. If a page can't be reached, Waypage can ask the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine for a copy.

Google Drive backups (only if you turn them on)

  • Waypage asks Google for one permission: drive.file. With it, Waypage can see and change only the files it made itself, which are its backups in a "Waypage" folder in your Drive. It can't open or read anything else in your Drive.
  • It reads your Google account's email address only so it can show you which account is connected.
  • Your backups go from your device straight to your own Google Drive. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • The sign-in is kept on your device. Disconnect Google Drive in Settings and Waypage gives its access back to Google. You can also remove it any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Your backups stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Waypage's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to make, list, restore and delete your backups. It isn't sold, used for advertising, or shown to anyone.

GitHub sync (only if you turn it on)

Sync keeps your library in a private GitHub repository that you own, using a token you make that reaches that repository only. The data goes from your device to GitHub and nowhere else.

Other things you choose to use

  • Feeds and newsletters. Your device fetches the feeds you follow. If you make a newsletter address, the emails go through Kill the Newsletter (kill-the-newsletter.com), which receives them so they can become a feed.
  • Translate runs on your device. Your device may download a language model, but the text of a clip you translate isn't sent anywhere. Translating a selected passage instead hands only that passage to Google Translate, and only when you tap it.
  • Report a problem shows you the whole report first and sends nothing on its own. If you choose to send it, it opens a GitHub issue that you post yourself. The report leaves out passwords, tokens and keys.
  • Pictures and videos that are only saved as links load from their site when you open them while online. A setting can stop this until you tap them.

Children

Waypage isn't aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page changes, with a new date. For questions, open an issue at github.com/danielnoam/waypage.
