Chapter 2: North of the Lighthouse

By R. Winslow

Ada found the map folded inside the lid of her father's sea chest, soft as cloth from handling. The harbour was drawn in brown ink, every rock and sandbar marked, and in the corner a small island she had never seen on any chart.

She spread it on the kitchen table and weighted the corners with teacups. Outside, the fog horn sounded twice, as it always did when the boats came in.

Her father had been a cartographer for forty years. He did not draw islands that were not there.

She did not sleep that night. By morning she had a plan, a borrowed boat and a list of questions for the only person who might know the answer.
