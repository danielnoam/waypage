Night Trains Are Coming Back

By Mara Ellison

For a while it looked as if the sleeper train would vanish, beaten by cheap flights and fast day trains. Now new routes open every year: Vienna to Paris, Brussels to Berlin, Stockholm to Hamburg.

The appeal is simple. You board after dinner, read for an hour, sleep, and step out in another country with the whole day ahead of you. No airport, no security queue, and a window full of countryside at breakfast.
