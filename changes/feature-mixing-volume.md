### Added
- Volume under Allow mixing, in Narrate and in Audiobook settings: 0 to 2× with normal (1×) in the middle, so the voice or book can sit under music or rise above it. Above 1× the Android app lifts the sound itself; on iPhone Narrate goes no louder than normal.
- While a transcript is being written, its words show up on the open page as each stretch is heard (every 10 to 20 seconds), not three minutes at a time.
- "Writing the transcript" in the phone's notifications while it writes, with how far the chapter has got.
- The chapter being written runs a light along its bar in the chapters list.

### Changed
- The audiobook player has no chapter name and hint above its bar any more; the chapters are in the list at the top.
- An audiobook added as a folder takes the folder's name, and the cover picture in the folder (cover.jpg, folder.png and the like).
