### Added
- Draw on a PDF with a pen: the pen in the reader bar (or D) turns on Draw for a PDF shown as its pages, scanned or not. A pen, a highlighter in five colours and an eraser (the pen's own eraser end works too), three sizes, Undo and Redo (Ctrl Z). The pen is wider as you press harder. On a touch screen only the pen draws (a pen's drag no longer scrolls the page, and its side button or eraser end erases), so a hand resting on the page leaves nothing and a finger still scrolls and pinches; Finger lets a finger or mouse draw (not beside a pen, where a hand is resting), and a computer starts with it on. Drawings stay as you left them at any zoom, are kept on this device with the clip, and show in ⋯ › Drawings, which lists the pages and saves a copy of the PDF with them as ink annotations (the PDF itself is never changed).
