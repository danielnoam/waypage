### Added
- Draw on a PDF with a pen: the pen in the reader bar (or D) turns on Draw for a PDF shown as its pages, scanned or not. A pen, a highlighter in five colours and an eraser (the pen's own eraser end works too), five sizes (a small scale opens from the size button, and [ and ] change it), four pen styles (ballpoint, marker, fountain pen and brush, from the style button), Undo and Redo (Ctrl Z). The pen is wider as you press harder. On a touch screen only the pen draws (a pen's drag no longer scrolls the page, and its side button or eraser end erases: on Android the phone's own pen reading is used, since a web page can't see the button), so a hand resting on the page leaves nothing and a finger still scrolls and pinches; Finger lets a finger or mouse draw (not beside a pen, where a hand is resting), and a computer starts with it on. Drawings stay as you left them at any zoom, are kept on this device with the clip, and show in ⋯ › Drawings, which lists the pages and saves a copy of the PDF with them as ink annotations (the PDF itself is never changed).
- ⋯ › Drawings has Try your pen: a pad that shows what the page and the phone each say of the pen's touch and buttons, and Start test, which asks for ten things in turn (a finger, the pen, its button, a resting hand) and records a log to save or copy and send.
- On a Samsung tablet, the pen's side button held and dragged erases (One UI sends no touch for it, so it's read natively), and the pen's touch no longer pans the page just after it comes in range.
