### Added
- Discover, in the sidebar: browse feeds, newsletters and Royal Road stories to follow, by kind (Sites for Royal Road, Feeds, Newsletters), and add one with a tap; swipe to move between the chips. A feed opens Add a feed, a story saves as a collection that gets its new chapters, and a newsletter (one that comes by email) copies your Newsletters address and opens its sign-up page. On the phone, Sites browses Royal Road and FictionPress as a grid, by list and genre, page after page, plus hand-picked web serials (Worm, The Wandering Inn and more); tap Royal Road's heading to open it. The search field at the top searches what's shown: the lists as you type, and the story site itself when you press search.
