### Added
- Find chapters and Website sync read a comix.to title's chapters. Each chapter there is posted by several scan groups, so Waypage reads one group's: the group in the link (`?group_id=`), else an official one, unless it is far behind the newest chapter, then the group with the newest. The collection stays with that group when it checks for new chapters. Android only; not built for iOS or the web copy.
- A comix.to chapter saves as a comic with all its pictures: every panel is found (the site shows them as you scroll) and kept on the device, so none says "Image loads when you're online". Android only; chapters saved before this have only their first panels and need saving again.

### Fixed
- A comix.to chapter no longer fails to save, or saves with only some of its pictures, when the site is slow to answer. Waypage scrolls the chapter until every picture is in (going back for any the site skipped), and a chapter that still comes up short says how many loaded and offers Try again, instead of being kept half done. Android only.
- comix.to: Find chapters and Website sync read every chapter of a title, from chapter 1 (they stopped at the newest 800), in a fraction of the time. The scan group picked is the most complete one (an official one when it has nearly all), and chapters it skipped come from another group, so a collection has no holes. A whole volume posted as one "chapter 0" is left out. Android only.
- comix.to: a chapter or list page the site's firewall turns away for a moment ("captcha required") is asked for again a few seconds later, instead of the chapter failing to save or the list coming back short. Long chapters (150+ pictures) get the time they need on a slower phone. Android only.
- comix.to: a title the site gave a new address is found again by its name, so its collection and Website sync carry on there instead of saying the page doesn't exist. Android only.
- Find chapters shows how far it is: a bar and the stage in words (which scan group, page 12 of 23, how many chapters so far, "The site asked to slow down. Trying again in 5 s"), under the button and as a card in Downloads with Stop. On Android, the same words and bar are in a notification, and it keeps reading while Waypage is put away; the chapters wait behind a "Found 510 chapters of The Gamer · Open" message. Android only for the notification.
- comix.to: a page the site turns away is seen at once and asked for again in 5 seconds (by loading its address: a second tap sends nothing) instead of stalling until a long timeout, and page turns are paced (one every 6 seconds after the first five) so most aren't turned away. A title read before (Website sync's check) reads only its newest page; a cover refresh no longer reads the whole list. The Gamer had stopped at chapter 393 for this reason. Android only.
