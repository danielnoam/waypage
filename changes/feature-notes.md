### Added
- Notes: a page of your own to draw and type on. Save with options › New note asks for blank, lines or squares paper (each shown beside its name) and opens a note with the pen ready. A note grows as you write: drawing on its last sheet adds another. Notes have their own part of the library, after Files, and are always a scroll.
- A note's Aa has only Layout (All notes or This note, margins, brightness slider, theme, Dark note) and Auto-scroll, which goes on to the next note.
- Typed text on a note: Add › Text puts words on the sheet in view, in three sizes. Select moves, copies, cuts and deletes it, and Edit text changes it, all undoable.
- The pen on a PDF asks to make a note from it: a copy of the PDF's pages you can draw on, in Notes. The PDF itself stays as it is, and drawings made on it before move into the note. ⋯ on a PDF has Make a note from this PDF too.
- ⋯ › Save as PDF on a note makes a PDF of its sheets with what is drawn, added and typed on them; the typed words can be searched.

### Changed
- Only a note can be drawn on. A PDF is no longer drawn on itself.
- Notes stay on this device for now: they are not synced or backed up.
