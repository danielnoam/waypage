### Added

- Discover has LibriVox: free public-domain audiobooks, browsed by most listened, newest or genre, or searched. On Android, Add the audiobook downloads its parts into your Library as one audiobook, its chapters named, to listen offline. Downloading shows in Downloads and can be stopped there or from the notification.
- When LibriVox knows the book it was read from on Project Gutenberg, its EPUB downloads too and the audiobook reads along with it (Read along with the book, on by default).
- In a browser, a LibriVox book offers its zip of MP3s instead.
