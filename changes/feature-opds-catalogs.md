### Added
- Catalogs, a new extension (Settings › Extensions): add book catalogs (OPDS) such as Calibre, Calibre-Web, COPS, Kavita or Komga, with a login if they need one. They get their own place in the sidebar, Catalogs: open their folders, search them, and add a book's EPUB, PDF or CBZ to the Library. Logins stay on the device.

### Changed
- Searching Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks and Wikisource from Discover › Sites works in the browser too, as browsing them already did.
- The app can reach plain http addresses, for book servers at home.
