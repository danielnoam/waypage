### Added
- Share reading activity: Settings › GitHub sync › Other apps. When it's on, sync writes what you're reading (title, author, how far, when you started and finished) and your minutes a day, reading and listening apart, to activity/reading.json in your own sync repo, so other apps you connect to it can use them. Off by default; each kind (books, audiobooks, comics, serials, articles) has its own switch.
- In other apps in a clip's or collection's ⋯ keeps it out of that file. Shown while sharing is on.
