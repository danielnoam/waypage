### Added
- Highlights on a PDF read from where it is: select words on its printed pages and tap Highlight, as in any clip. They go with the clip's other highlights (its Highlights sheet, the Highlights place, Copy all and Save all highlights), take a colour and a note, and show in Show as text when its words are found there.

### Removed
- The Highlights button in the reader for a comic or a scanned PDF, which have no words to highlight.
